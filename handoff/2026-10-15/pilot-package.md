## Segment, JTBD, and eligibility

**Provisional segment:** a person who explicitly owns a small business or is pursuing a business/project idea without a team, recently completed or is actively completing a consequential task outside their expertise, used or expects to use external information or help, and can safely reconstruct the task without sharing confidential materials.

**Job story:** When I need to move a business decision forward in an area outside my expertise, I want to compare verifiable external sources in a concise analysis that exposes limitations and unanswered questions, so I can choose the next step without mistaking a draft for expert validation.

**Screen in:** explicit owner/founder/solo idea status; one-person/no-team context or clear personal ownership of the project; a real task from the last 90 days or an active task; named decision or deliverable; relevant expertise gap; permission to discuss the case without confidential inputs. Ask separately whether the person is willing to discuss it and whether they consent to the specific research session and data handling.

**Screen out or route elsewhere:** imagined-only task for the observed-barrier route; no decision depending on the work; expert readily available and already used; confidential or regulated material that cannot be safely abstracted; high-stakes legal, tax, medical, investment, or safety decision requiring authorized professional judgment. Such domains may be discussed only as process research with clear escalation to qualified sources; the product must not imply professional advice.

Three stable user groups are **not** evidenced. Use the following as three sequential task hypotheses within one proto-segment, not as three established segments:

## ZIP deliverable contract

Each exported run ZIP contains at minimum a user-facing result, the sources supporting its external factual claims, and project/run/build metadata. Do not include raw form responses, contact details, unapproved source files, recordings, or identifiable quotes.

```text
coreteams_<project-id>_<task-id>_<run-id>_<phase>_<build-version>.zip
  manifest.json
  result.md
  sources.csv
  metadata.json
```

`result.md` is the user result and contains the required sections defined in `SKILL.md`. `sources.csv` has `source_id,title,publisher,author,published_at,accessed_at,url,claim_ids,source_type,limitations`; each cited external factual claim maps to one or more source IDs. Keep source excerpts short and within license/copyright limits. For internal or participant-provided sources, include only an approved description and permission status; do not package the underlying file unless the participant explicitly authorized it.

`metadata.json` records `project_id`, `task_id`, `run_id`, `phase`, `build_version`, `environment`, `created_at` with timezone, `operator_id`, `participant_id` pseudonym if applicable, `input_scope` (descriptive, no confidential data), `consent_status` and consent-text version (not the contact record), `result_schema_version`, and `limitations`. `manifest.json` lists every payload file except `manifest.json` itself (no self-hash), media type, byte size, SHA-256 digest, and schema version. Do not put personal contact data in metadata.

ZIP checks: required files exist and are non-empty except an explicitly empty-but-schema-valid source table; metadata IDs/build/phase match the filename and each other; every external factual assertion has a resolvable source ID and URL; all URLs and access dates are present; there are no orphan source IDs; files open as UTF-8; manifest hashes match the archive members; archive contains no contact columns, secrets, raw responses, temporary files, or unapproved source documents. A result with insufficient evidence must say so in its body and metadata. Do not fabricate a citation to make the archive pass.
