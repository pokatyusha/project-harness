# Video workflow

## Goal

Deliver a CTF video through scenario, generation prompts, asset assembly, and review.

## Inputs

Audience, duration, format, approved claims, brand rules, and available source assets.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the brief and factual claims.
2. Draft scenario and shot list.
3. Create prompts and an assembly checklist.
4. Request approval before publishing or external distribution.

## Done

An approved scenario, reproducible prompts, asset list, and review result are stored.
