# Presentations workflow

## Goal

Prepare a defence narrative, slide structure, texts, and evidence aligned to the rubric.

## Inputs

Defence date, rubric, required format, project evidence, and reviewers' feedback.

## Procedure

1. Confirm rubric and timing.
2. Use `presentation-visual-motion` after content boundaries are known to set the design direction and motion plan.
3. Draft slide-by-slide message and evidence.
4. Flag unverified claims and missing proof.
5. Use `monetization-unit-model` where a business-model slide needs a bounded value or unit-economics claim.
6. Use `evidence-artifact-review` against the rubric before export or delivery.

## Done

Every slide has a purpose, evidence source, speaker text, and review status.
