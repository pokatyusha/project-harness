# Design-thinking experiment

## Trigger

Use when Direction 2.5 or Pilot users has an uncertain user problem and needs to test a concept before treating it as a specification or feature. It is not a substitute for real research, consent, or an approved product decision.

## Inputs

- Decision to inform and a bounded target user or situation
- Existing evidence, assumptions, and constraints
- Safe test method, owner, and consent boundary

## Procedure

1. Start with `templates/design-thinking-experiment.md`; distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
2. Describe the user context, need, and desired progress in a point-of-view statement.
3. Turn the problem into one testable “How might we” question; do not start from a preferred feature.
4. Generate alternative concepts without ranking them as facts, then choose the smallest artefact that can test the riskiest assumption.
5. Define a concrete hypothesis, signal, disconfirming signal, method, and decision owner before running the test.
6. Record what changed after the test. Route a supported product change through `journey-evidence-to-backlog` or `direction-2-5-spec-readiness` as appropriate.

## Boundaries

- Do not substitute future-intent answers, simulated users, or AI output for observed behaviour.
- Do not contact people, collect personal data, or run a test without the required owner confirmation and consent process.
- A prototype is evidence-gathering material, not proof that a feature is accepted or production-ready.

## Done

The experiment records the decision, evidence and assumptions, problem framing, test artefact, success and disconfirming signals, result, and next decision.

## Ownership and revision

The research or product owner approves the test boundary. Revise when the decision, target situation, evidence, or consent boundary changes.
