# Pilot segmentation and JTBD

## Trigger

Use to turn a research question into a recruitable segment and a falsifiable need statement before pilot-user outreach.

## Inputs

- Product or research hypothesis
- Existing evidence and known uncertainty
- Recruitment and consent constraints

## Procedure

1. Start from `templates/pilot-segment-jtbd.md` and name the decision the segment must inform.
2. Describe the segment with observable context and eligibility criteria; mark every unsupported statement as an assumption.
3. Use 5W questions to expose the user, circumstance, trigger, alternatives, outcome, and timing.
4. Write one Job Story in the form: “When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected progress].”
5. Define interview or prototype-test questions that could disprove the segment or Job Story.
6. Prepare a candidate-list specification only. Obtain owner confirmation before finding contacts, inviting people, or collecting personal data.

## Boundaries

- A persona is a research-backed prototype, not an invented biography.
- Do not segment only by demographics when behaviour, context, or need is the decision-relevant criterion.
- Do not apply RFM, ABC/XYZ, or other data-driven segmentation without the required dataset and a stated method.
- Do not store raw participant identifiers in this repository.

## Done

The record contains the evidence level, eligibility criteria, 5W view, one Job Story, disconfirming questions, and consent boundary.

## Ownership and revision

The project owner approves recruitment scope and outreach separately. Revise when evidence changes eligibility, the situation, or the desired progress.
