## Interview and form boundary

- When a person has completed a relevant real task or used the tested flow, reconstruct the actual case and identify time-consuming barriers by stage: source discovery, selection, reading or extraction, synthesis, validation, and decision.
- When a person has not had that interaction, ask about the ideal result and conditions that would make them try the product. Treat this as a counterfactual expectation; the team must compare it with product capability and choose a later test.
- Future intent, an imagined workflow, and an unrun test do not establish an observed barrier, demand, or product success.
